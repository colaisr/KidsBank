## Leo visual (6–10)

Chibi mascot pattern (Duolingo / Pokémon Kids): **head ~60%** of silhouette, **large eyes with white highlights**, round muzzle, pink cheeks, fluffy mane puffs — reads friendly and tappable, not realistic.

Palette: amber mane `#F59E0B` / cream face `#FFEFB8` — aligned with young card skin **leo**.

## Motion (3)

1. **Breathe** — gentle scale + float on body (2.8s loop).
2. **Blink** — eyelids scale on eyes (~4.5s loop).
3. **React** — bounce + wiggle on tap or Play/Talk (0.65s one-shot).

Dock adds soft **glow pulse**. All disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
